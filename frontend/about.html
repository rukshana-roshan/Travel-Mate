How TravelMate Works

Designed for clarity, convenience, and realistic travel scheduling.

1. Explore & Filter

Browse our curated catalog of 10+ verified destinations within 25km of Atulugama. Filter by Beaches, Nature, Heritage, Temples, or Adventure and review key facts.

2. Shortlist Your Stops

Click "Add to Trip" on any attraction card. TravelMate prevents duplicates and compiles your personalized destination shortlist.

3. Automated Schedule

Set your morning start and evening end times. Our deterministic nearest-neighbour algorithm schedules arrival times, visits, travel buffers, and lunch breaks.
