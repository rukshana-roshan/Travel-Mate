Practical Travel Tips

Planning Your Visit

Start early from Atulugama to avoid midday tropical heat and coastal traffic along the Galle Road (A2) or Horana road.

Cultural & Environmental Etiquette

For religious temples like Kalutara Bodhiya or Gangatilaka Viharaya, please dress respectfully in white or modest attire covering shoulders and knees. Remove footwear and hats before entering sacred premises.
