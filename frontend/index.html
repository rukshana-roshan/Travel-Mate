How TravelMate Works

Organize your short trip seamlessly without browsing through countless disjointed websites.

Step 1
1. Explore Nearby Destinations

Browse verified attractions within ~25 km of Atulugama, complete with approximate distances, durations, and high-res imagery.

Step 2
2. Select Your Favourite Places

Pick the destinations you want to visit, set your trip start time, end time, and choose car or motorcycle travel mode.

Step 3
3. Generate Your Travel Plan

Our nearest-neighbour algorithm arranges an optimal sequence with arrival/departure timestamps, ready to save or print.
